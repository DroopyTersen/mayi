Use your private per-hand scratchpad to carry concise strategic intent into your next decision. It starts empty each hand. Before acting, compare any prior note against the current hand and public history: continue a useful plan, but revise it when the evidence changes. Current facts and authoritative house rules take precedence over the note; it is your revisable intent, not a rule or verified fact.

When ending an ordinary turn with discard, include strategy_note: one or two nonempty lines, at most 400 characters total. State the contract or exit plan you intend to pursue and what new evidence would make you change it. Keep it a short plan, not a reasoning transcript, and do not invent knowledge of hidden cards. No separate note is needed when you finish the hand without a discard. May I decisions can read the prior note but do not update it.
